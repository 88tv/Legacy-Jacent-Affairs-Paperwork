[color=#886e6a][head=3]Cargo Technician[/head][/color]
[ ] 1a. One stack of each material (Glass, plastic, steel) was not delivered to [color=#b18644]Cargo[/color], [color=#5b97bc]Medical[/color], [color=#cb0000]Security[/color], or [color=#f39f27]Engineering[/color]. 
[ ] 1b. Resources were not delivered to each department listed in 1a at least four times during the shift.
[ ] 2a. Security items were ordered by a [color=#b18644]Cargo Technician[/color] without being through a [color=#cb0000]security[/color] member's order slip. 
[ ] 2b. An order involving armor or weaponry was approved without a [color=#cb0000]security[/color] member's order slip or without approval from the [color=#b18644]Quartermaster[/color] or [color=#cb0000]Head of Security[/color].
[ ] 2c. [color=#cb0000]Security[/color] orders were approved without personnel already being on the Automated Trade Station to pick them up.
[ ] 3a. [color=#b18644]Cargo[/color] failed to deposit bounty slips at appropriate desks.
[ ] 3b. [color=#b18644]Cargo[/color] failed to construct a lockbox for any bounty being worked on.
[ ] 3c. [color=#b18644]Cargo[/color] failed to continue work on fulfilling bounties.
[ ] 4. [color=#b18644]Cargo[/color] ordered items for a non-work purpose without the [color=#b18644]Quartermaster[/color]'s approval.
[ ] 5. While there were no [color=#b18644]Cargo Technicians[/color] willing to accept the position of Mail Clerk, the Mail Teleporter was online.
[ ] 6. An order approved on the ordering console failed to be delivered within 15 minutes of approval.
[ ] 7. Non-work use orders from other departments were approved without being stamped by the appropriate Department Head.
[ ] 8a. [color=#b18644]Cargo[/color] failed to hold a recipient-signed copy of each delivery made on the station.
[ ] 8b. The recipient-signed copies of delivery blue slips were not provided to the [color=#b18644]Quartermaster[/color] or left in a folder designated by the [color=#b18644]Quartermaster[/color].
[ ] 9. The crew were demanded trades or compensation to approve orders, with exception made for spesos transferred for issues of missing funds.
